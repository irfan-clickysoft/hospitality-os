## What to try first (about 15 minutes each)
1. **A late arrival with missing details.** Today → a guest arriving → Check in. What stops you? Is it clear what to do?
2. **The room is not ready.** Move the guest to another room from the side panel. Ask housekeeping to clean the room first.
3. **Check-out with money owing.** Today → Leaving today → open a guest → settle the bill → check out.
4. **On a phone**: open the address, sign in as `bilal@` (housekeeping) or `gate@`, switch to Urdu from the person icon.
5. **Press `?`** on any screen to see the keyboard shortcuts, and `Ctrl K` to search.

## How to give feedback (please be specific)
Send one message per thing, with:
- **Who you were signed in as** and **which screen** (the address in the browser is enough).
- **What you tried to do**, **what you expected**, and **what happened**.
- A **screenshot** if something looks wrong.
- For Urdu: the text that is wrong and what it should say.

Things that are especially useful: anything that took you more than a few seconds to work out, anything you would have done differently in real life, and any word (English or Urdu) that staff would not use.
