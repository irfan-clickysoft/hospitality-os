# 11 — Observation Week Checklist (Sunset Boulevard)

**Purpose:** learn how the property really operates before the user interface is frozen, and collect the data the system needs.
**When:** this week, as soon as dates are agreed. **Who:** Irfan A Rehman (decisions) + one or two developers/product people; Boulevard's manager, night-shift front-desk officer/auditor, a housekeeper, the cook, a guard, and the central bookings manager and accountant for one session each.
**Rules:** observe, don't change their process; ask permission before photographing screens or documents; never copy guests' ID documents or card details — blank/sample forms and redacted exports only.

## 1. Day-by-day plan
| Day | Focus | With whom |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front desk day shift: arrivals, walk-ins, payments, phone and WhatsApp bookings | Manager, front-desk officer |
| 2 | Housekeeping round: room turnover, inspection, lost & found, minibar, laundry | Housekeepers, manager |
| 3 | Kitchen + room service + meeting room (hourly / full-day hires) | Cook, kitchen support |
| 4 | **Night shift**: the full end-of-day routine, cash count, reports, tomorrow's arrivals | Night officer / auditor |
| 5 | Central team: bookings manager (Booking.com, corporate, agents), accountant (invoices, receivables, tax), GM (reports) | Central staff |
| 6 | Gate: guards' log, visitor/vehicle procedures, ID checks, police/authority reporting | Guards, manager |
| 7 | Review: walk through the first screen designs with the same people | All |

## 2. What to watch and record
**Reservations:** channels used (Booking.com, direct, phone, WhatsApp, corporate, agents) · how a booking is recorded today · time to create one · how changes/cancellations/no-shows are handled · how unpaid Booking.com bookings are followed up · how often rooms go empty after no-shows (last 6–12 months, if available).
**Check-in / check-out:** documents asked for · who records what · how long check-in takes · how rooms are assigned · deposits taken · registration card/signature · keys · what slows the desk down.
**Money:** payment methods in practice (cash, bank transfer, card machine, wallet) · where each is recorded · cash handling at shift end · who counts · how differences are handled · deposits, refunds, discounts, complimentary stays and who approves them · how bills are split between company and guest · invoice formats used today (collect a blank/sample).
**Corporate:** list of corporate clients, agreed rates, credit limits, payment terms (15/30 days), when invoices are sent, how payments and tax deductions at source are matched · sample statement.
**Night routine:** every step in order · what is checked · what is posted · what reports are produced and who reads them · how the business date changes · what goes wrong.
**Housekeeping:** how rooms are assigned · what staff are told and how · inspection · issues reported to maintenance · language spoken · phones used · connectivity in the building.
**Kitchen / services:** menu and prices · how orders reach the kitchen · how charges reach the room bill · laundry, transport, pickup/drop and how they are charged.
**Meeting room:** price list (hourly, half-day, full-day) · how it is booked and paid · set-up time between bookings.
**Gate:** what guards record · who they must report to · what a guard needs to see about arriving guests · authority reporting formats and frequency.
**Environment:** internet reliability and outages · power cuts and backup · devices available (PCs, tablets, phones, printers, scanners) · Wi-Fi coverage for staff phones.

## 4. Questions to ask every role
1. What do you do first when you arrive? What is the most repeated task?
2. What takes longest or goes wrong most often?
3. What do you write down twice?
4. What do you wish the system told you?
5. What would stop you using a new system?

## 5. Outputs (within two days of finishing)
1. One-page "day in the life" per role.
2. Updated workflow diagrams where reality differs from the documents.
3. A prioritised list of design changes and quick wins.
4. First-screen designs for: reservations list, room rack, check-in, folio/payment, housekeeping app, night audit.
5. The data-collection checklist above, completed or with owners and dates for what is missing.
